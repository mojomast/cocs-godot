# The Quiet Relay — first-playthrough timing sheet

The requested 5–10 minute duration is a **design target**, not a result inferred
from dimensions, a mission timer, scripted traversal or automated kills.

Record build revision, platform/GPU/renderer, graphics settings, difficulty,
input device, prior FPS experience and whether the player has seen the map.
Use a fresh level start. Keep retry attempts in total active time; record pause
time separately. Do not erase slow/failed attempts from the sample.

For each level note:

- Could the next landmark/objective be found without instructions?
- Was the objective readable on arrival, during combat and after clearing it?
- Which enemy silhouettes were confused? At what approximate distance?
- Did attack warnings give enough time to act? Which hit felt unexplained?
- Did a flank/supply route provide a useful choice?
- Was there an excessively empty traversal or repetitive encounter?
- Did death/retry restore a fair checkpoint and preserve solved progress?
- Did the exit clearly lead into the next chapter? Did the ending resolve the
  archive/network story?
- Did movement, aiming, firing, subtitles and UI remain comfortable/readable?

Initial acceptance target: median normal-difficulty first-time completion
between 300 and 600 seconds per level across at least five players, with no
mandatory clock failures or objective softlocks. Record distributions and
outliers, not only the average. Adjust route/encounter content from observation,
not by adding idle wait timers to force the duration.

Until these observations exist, release notes must label duration **targeted**.
