## Known limitations

The distance-based gait remains frame-rate dependent below 20 FPS. Original
mesh-cost reporting omits the new near/mid armor overlays. Hardware performance,
human animation feel, graphical Windows execution and competitive balance have
not been established. The canonical full hosted suite was not rerun for this
focused preview; prior consolidated crew/Horde-upgrade fixture failures remain
unresolved. New biome contracts were invoked directly rather than registered
as canonical gates.
