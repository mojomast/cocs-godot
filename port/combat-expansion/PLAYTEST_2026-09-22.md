# Playtest — combat expansion build (`combat-expansion-2026-09-22`)

Your part of the plan. Nothing here needs a terminal unless you want the benchmark
number; the first three sections are just playing the build and telling me what you see.

Download: https://github.com/mojomast/cocs-godot/releases/tag/combat-expansion-2026-09-22
Extract the ZIP, open `cocs-native-windows`, run **Native Deathmatch.cmd**.

## 1. First impressions (2 minutes)

- Does each of the six maps look like a distinct place? (Prism Foundry, Aurora Basin,
  Cinder Array, Lacuna Court, Vermilion Fold, Nacre Engine)
- Do the operators look like the source game's characters? Any obvious geometry bugs,
  warped limbs, or weapons floating away from hands?
- Anything that reads as broken rather than "not finished yet"?

## 2. Weapons and aiming (5 minutes)

- **RMB** into ADS on several weapons: is the sight picture centred and usable, and does
  each weapon look clearly different from the others?
- Fire a few, reload, swap weapons: do bolts, magazines, casings and heat effects read
  correctly, or does any of it look wrong or distracting?
- Do remote bots' weapons read as the same objects you're holding?

## 3. Effects and blood (5 minutes)

- Kill a bot at close range and at distance: blood spurts, death burst, and marks on the
  floor **and walls**. Too much, too little, wrong colour, or wrong place?
- Take damage: is the damage-direction indicator helpful, is the low-health state
  visible without being annoying?
- Press **F10** and read the metrics panel: it should look tidy, not run off screen.

## 4. Performance — the number I most need (5 minutes)

On each of two maps (one arena, one identity map):

1. Play for ~30 seconds at the default **High** and note roughly how it feels.
2. Press **F9** to cycle to **Extreme**, fire a rocket into a group, and note whether it
   stays smooth.
3. Report: resolution, the map, whether Extreme was smooth / playable / stuttery /
   unplayable, and anything specific that hitched (map load, first shot, first death).

A benchmark mode that prints real median/p95 frame times is being added in the next
build; until then your impressions plus resolution are the useful signal.

## 5. Known UI issues — please confirm or refute

These are inherited defects being fixed right now, so tell me if you still see them:

- A combat setup/strategy popup that obstructs the view or traps input
- The Horde scoreboard covering health/ammo at small resolutions
- A wrong player-count label (e.g. "4 players")
- Oversized result pickup captions

## 6. Optional: the terminal routes

```bat
Play.cmd --experience=native-dm --map=lacuna-court
Play.cmd --experience=native-dm --map=vermilion-fold
Play.cmd --help
```

## What to send back

1. Resolution and GPU/driver if you know them
2. Per-map: Extreme smooth / playable / stuttery / unplayable
3. The single worst-feeling thing about the build
4. Whether the new maps and the detailed weapons are good enough to keep, or what the
   biggest visual problem is
5. Any of the four UI defects from section 5 that you still hit

That set tells me whether the next push should be Domination/Horde content, performance
work, or visual polish — which is exactly the decision this build exists to inform.
