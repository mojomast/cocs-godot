# Single-player feel pass — 2026-10-01

## Research actually consulted

All links accessed **2026-10-01**. These are developer-authored accounts or direct developer interviews; design applications below are our decisions, not claimed quotations or validated universal formulas.

1. **Kurt Loudy / Jake Campbell, id Software, GDC 2018: [Embracing Push Forward Combat in DOOM](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1024940/Embracing-Push-Forward-).** Retrieved the session abstract, not a full video transcript. Its explicit emphasis is aggression, speed and obtaining resources from adversaries. Application: small bounded near-kill salvage and fragile light robots reward closing distance. We retain forgiving regeneration because this campaign is not DOOM's resource-only economy.
2. **Jaime Griesemer, interview dated 2011-07-14: [Half-Minute Halo](https://www.engadget.com/2011-07-14-half-minute-halo-an-interview-with-jaime-griesemer.html).** Read the full interview. Griesemer specifically corrects the idea of repeating identical 30 seconds: changing enemies, combinations, environments and weapons supplies context. He describes a 3-second / 30-second / 3-minute hierarchy and letting a player retreat to recover. Application: discrete attack warnings/bursts/rest, limited simultaneous shooters, alternating compact fights and set pieces; restoration runs *during* combat, with optional risky console bypass rather than another post-clear wait.
3. **Jan Willem Nijman / Rami Ismail, Vlambeer, 2012-05-09: [Gun Godz postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/postmortem-vlambeer-s-i-gun-godz-i-).** Read the full primary-source postmortem. Specific lessons: weak-but-dangerous enemies keep pace; every weapon needs a useful situation; level geometry shapes fights; the Rat King's unique invulnerability was hard to communicate even with eyes and audio. Application: retain weapon damage/range identities, reduce layered enemy durability, use breakable guard and finite boss recovery instead of invulnerability, show physical hit recoil and lowered guard. Existing per-weapon recoil/muzzle/tracer/impact work is retained; add a delayed mechanical return to ballistic reports, not blanket louder audio or camera shake.
4. **Glenn Fiedler, 2004-06-10: [Fix Your Timestep!](https://gafferongames.com/post/fix_your_timestep/).** Read full article. Simulation rate and render rate must be decoupled, with bounded steps and a visual reconstruction. Application: keep authority's fixed ticks; repair the time basis used by the visual camera rather than modifying input TTL or physics assertions.
5. **Glenn Fiedler, 2014-11-30: [Snapshot Interpolation](https://gafferongames.com/post/snapshot_interpolation/).** Read full article. Even regularly sent snapshots arrive in irregular batches; sequencing matters; extrapolation cannot predict collision reliably. Application: use simulation time plus transmitted velocity for campaign camera motion; reject older simulation samples, consume advancing same-arrival samples, stop horizontal extrapolation on authority-confirmed collision. Keep the 50 ms extrapolation ceiling, epoch/death resets, direct look control and stale-input safeguards.

Supplementary discovery: Nijman's [Art of Screenshake, INDIGO 2013](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJdEqssNZ-U) was found through search, but not watched/transcribed; no implementation claim depends on it. An RPS interview fetch returned HTTP 403 and was not used as evidence.

## Code-level root causes

* `game/enemy-types.mjs`: Bulwark 230 HP + 85 armor + 70% frontal reduction, while Sentinel contributes 45 temporary shield each 3.4 s (cap 90). This compounds durability far beyond the visible chassis. The pass uses 110+20 HP/armor, a 45% breakable front plate; Sentinel becomes 78+18 with 16 shields, cap 24, every 6 seconds.
* `game/operator-verbs.mjs`: multiplayer direct hits cap at **90% target max HP** (also 90 absolute), so even a Rail Lance leaves a full-health Scrapper sliver. Campaign's explicit authoritative light-chassis rupture rule resolves a decisive hit rather than changing the frozen multiplayer primitive.
* `game/bots.mjs`: generic bots directly call `match.fire`, grenade, and operator power logic while computing input. Merely filtering returned `fire` input cannot constrain this. Campaign overrides these methods: no inherited NPC grenade/operator-power surprises, explicit range/warning/burst/rest, 2/3/4 active attack lanes by difficulty. Mortars use their readable source artillery attack instead of an extra gun.
* Player starts at **100 health / 5 armor** in the inherited ChatGPT stats; every robot can overlap attacks. Normal now starts 140/60 with a 34-damage crossfire budget per 300 ms, before armor. Sustained pressure remains lethal; difficulty changes forgiveness/pressure, not enemy HP inflation.
* `game/singleplayer.mjs:updateHealthRegen`: firing pushes the regen delay back to two seconds. Campaign now recovers 16 HP/s after 3 damage-free seconds on Normal, including while returning fire; armor recovers only to 40 after 5 seconds. Near kills give at most 8 health and 6 armor, never resurrect or exceed caps.
* `enemies.mjs`: deployment selected the first supported candidate, often making a clump with no role-specific position. Candidates now rank toward flank/rear/front formation sites, remain floor/clearance/reachability checked, and stay at least 7 m from the player. Warden retains priority for reviewed authored spawn points.
* `match.mjs`: restoration waited for every guard to die before progress could begin. It now permits early activation and progress while fighting. Interaction consoles additionally offer a guard-network bypass, without skipping the living-enemy or checkpoint gate.
* `world/local_motion.gd` and `world/session.gd`: campaign used receive time to infer velocity; a drained batch could hit the 40 m/s limit. Campaign now provides source time and actual snapshot velocity. Stopped axes discard camera correction that would coast through collision. No look, aim, movement authority, TTL or collision geometry changes.

## Scope / integration contract

Campaign balance lives only in `port/native-campaign/{feel,match,enemies,missions}.mjs`; source `game/**`, `server/**` and the reproducible core adapter stay byte-identical. Parent's cheat wrapper attaches after match creation; disabled cheats call these normal methods. Parent owns authority/debug/menu wiring and `godot/campaign/demo.gd`.

Shared camera helper changes apply sequencing improvements to source-clock consumers; explicit authority velocity is supplied only for campaign. It uses no grounded/floor assumption, so debug flight remains compatible. Shared audio adds a peak-normalized, existing Effects-bus mechanical layer; volumes/mute still apply. Robot hit/guard animation is campaign-only. Existing reduced-motion first-person presentation remains responsible for weapon kick; no camera shake is added.

Horde remains another agent's ownership. Later recommendations: audit shield stacking and inherited generic attacks first; adopt role-specific warnings and bounded near-kill salvage only after its own authority tests. Do not copy campaign's small active-lane budget blindly into Horde's crowd fantasy.

## Measurement and acceptance boundaries

`node port/singleplayer-feel/measure.mjs` measures all ten primary weapons against all six robot roles (plus Bulwark front/rear) through source clamp/armor/death primitives. It is an **ideal connected-hit envelope**, excluding misses, falloff, projectile travel, reloads, support pulses and human decisions. It reports authored route lengths, a traversal-only lower bound at 8.6 m/s, and actual encounter rosters. Neither those lower bounds nor teleport/kill checkpoint tests validate the 300–600 second human chapter target.

`feel.test.mjs` verifies survivable simultaneous bursts, lethal sustained damage, difficulty ordering, finite guard breaks/stagger immunity, range/attack-lane warnings/rest, recovery while firing, no corpse salvage farming, risky console bypass gates, combat-overlapping restoration and boss recovery vulnerability.

`godot/tests/campaign/feel_motion.gd` exercises actual presentation code under regular and irregular batched snapshots, source ordering, exact same-arrival packets, collision stops, stairs, legitimate correction, death and epoch reset. Engine execution is pending the parent's exclusive-slot grant; Node results cannot substitute for it. Existing first-person recoil, audio, campaign visual and native route checks will be used during that bounded slot.
