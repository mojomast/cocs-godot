## New-map and asset quality bar

Blender generation must produce editable masters and real exported assets with
provenance. A map is not complete just because its route tests pass: inspect actual
eye-level architecture and revise sparse fields of isolated primitives. Require
distinct built districts, substantial interiors, structural depth, purposeful
cover, coherent materials, landmarks and source-traversable crosslinks. Triangle
or object counts are budgets, not substitutes for architectural quality.

Map recipes drive both source geometry and native art. Source support chooses the
highest walkable floor at X/Z: use non-overlapping terraces and non-walkable ceilings
instead of unsupported stacked decks. Tall wall quads can fail actor collision;
emit individual wall triangles and test actual sustained body contact plus rays.
Visible openings must remain open. Validate vehicle clearances where relevant.
Use standalone generation without duplicating already-expanded overhead slabs.

Map publication requires inspected overview/eye-level imagery, ordinary-input
hosted objective journeys, native/source collider agreement, readable 1280×800
and 760×520/UI150 HUDs, measured budgets and a representative walkthrough with
actual captured cadence recorded. Parent owns shared catalogs and packaging.
