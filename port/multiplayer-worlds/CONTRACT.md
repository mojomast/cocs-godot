# Multiplayer worlds: shared authoring contract (checkpoint 1)

This is a **port-owned expansion**, independent of the locked nine-map source catalog and the existing eight native DM maps. The source `game/**`, `server/**`, and `port/contracts/map-selection.json` bytes remain locked. A new map is a distinct authored playable arena, not an alias for an old ID.

## Authority and recipe boundary

* Commit `godot/multiplayer_worlds/generated/<id>.json` as the canonical **gameplay** recipe, with `schemaVersion: 1`, unique `id`/`name`, `geometryHash = SHA256(canonicalArenaJSON(arena))`, `arena`, `spawnPoints`, `routes`, `palette`, and presentation `art`. The canonical hash algorithm is `port/native-arenas/schema.mjs`; recursive object keys sort, array order remains fixed. IDs are lowercase kebab-case and individually allowlisted by the port-owned catalog. A client never supplies a recipe, filename, or runtime path.
* `arena` is consumed by the locked `game/core.mjs` `Match` constructor via the already-proven port-owned pre-construction accessor pattern (`port/native-arenas/match.mjs`, `port/native-identity-zones/match.mjs`). Its `bounds`, `spawns`, `teamSpawns`, `navNodes`, `pickups`, `blocks`, `terrain.surfaces`, `terrain.walls`, and mode-specific objective fields are gameplay truth, **not** decorative Blender mesh. Godot must render and collide from this very recipe; glTF/GLB is art-only and cannot create extra walkable support, opaque cover, or invisible impassable walls. Metadata must validate and the client's loaded hash must equal the authority's `start.geometryHash` before input is accepted.
* Terrain support lives in `terrain.surfaces[]` (indexed triangles with 3D vertices, `walkable:true`), ramps/stairs included, with slope no greater than the source `terrain.maxSlope`. `blocks[]` (x/z/w/d/baseY/h) and `terrain.walls[]` represent authoritative collision and rays. Overhead decoration, glass, rails and set dressing belong to `art[]` only unless their gameplay collision is explicitly represented in arena. Every spawn, nav node, pickup, flag, objective zone, payload waypoint, vehicle pad and sports checkpoint must have a valid supported height and a reachable route. Multi-level paths need real climbable support and nav links, not only a vertical visual staircase; verify `game/terrain.mjs` `terrainSupportAt`, `game/core.mjs` `floorAt`, `walkEdge`, `obstructed`, and `matchNavigation` on exported coordinates.
* Blender source in `tools/godot-multiplayer/<lane>/` uses deterministic exporter with a checked-in `.blend` master and GLB art. Decorative assets are named with stable semantic IDs and material slots; batch/instance repeated modules, track triangle/draw budgets and preserve full-height walkable clearance. World lane owns nonurban recipes/art; urban lane owns the shared parser, registry, renderer, packaging/options/menu and urban recipes/art. Horde's local runtime/catalog is owned by its lane; its public integration export is registered here only after its handoff.

## Mode authoring fields and actual source consumers

All modes are the source `game/config.mjs` rules and source `Match` mechanics, with **authored** geometry. `arena.objectiveZones` powers domination/holdout/koth/uplink/assault through `game/objectives.mjs` (confirm per-mode shape before emitting). `arena.teamSpawns` gives two supported spawn pools. `arena.flagSpawns`/`objectiveNodes` supply CTF (inspect constructor defaults); `arena.payloadRoute`/checkpoints supply payload through `game/payload.mjs`. `arena.race`, vehicle spawn/roads, and soccer fields must match the source vehicle/sports readers; LATTICE has a separate map/world source contract. Recipes must declare `supportedModes` in the port-owned registry, never a wish-list mode. World lane should provide purpose-built vehicle/race/soccer/lattice geometry plus precise mode fields rather than put a race alias on an infantry arena.

The new experience will launch its own static-catalog port-owned authority over protocol v3 with an explicit `mapId/mode` pair. Multi-human lobby must use the source match and source objective state for **both** host and guests, including late join and restart. Local bot fill uses source bot seats. No existing local-only native-DM or identity-zones authority is claimed to support a second human: those implementations explicitly bind one socket and actor 0. The package/scene routes are expansion-specific and must reject an unsupported map/mode before launching; existing source routes remain intact.

## Delivery and checks per map

Produce a matrix of `(id, mode, scene, authority, objective fields)` and verified launch commands. Static checks: schema/hash; exact shared support/render transforms; spawn safety and height; nav connectivity at every elevation; route clearance; LOS/cover; team travel distances; objective capture/flag/payload/sports transition on source match. After heavy-slot grant: deterministic Blender export, Godot import/package, ground/interior/roof and objective screenshots, a two-client host/guest objective completion/late-join/restart proof. A mode is not listed as playable until its own route, collision, authority and client presentation pass.

## Discovered baseline roster (2026-10-01)

`tools/godot-package/options.mjs`: combat/lobby DM, TDM, Instagib, Rockets; arms-race; zones Domination, KOTH, Uplink, Holdout; Assault; objectives CTF, Payload; Combined Arms; sports Puma Race, Puma Soccer; LATTICE board/world COCS and COCS Coop. Horde is handed to the Horde lane. Baseline `godot/objectives/demo.gd` hardcodes only Tidal CTF/Sunscar Payload and `godot/zone_modes/demo.gd` validates locked catalog modes: the expansion needs explicit routes/scenes, not just new option strings. Baseline `port/native-arenas/authority.mjs` and `port/native-identity-zones/authority.mjs` are one-human loopback implementations and cannot prove multi-human behavior.
