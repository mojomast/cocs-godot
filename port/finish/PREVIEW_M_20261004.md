## Source fix before the build

`93ad708c` regenerates `port/multiplayer-worlds/derived/core.mjs` after
`91f58a1c` changed `game/core.mjs` (infantry air control / landing slides) without
refreshing the mechanically derived world copy. The generator's `--check` (run by
`build.py`) failed on the stale derivative; regeneration is the prescribed fix and
the diff is exactly the 32/10-line source change. Committed and pushed.
