## Research findings to verify through implementation

- Distance-synchronized gait currently scales horizontal stance travel by
  normalized speed again, permitting foot slide at slower speeds. There are also
  two pose solves in the live path. Fix the stance invariant and establish one
  gait owner, then test strafe/turn/start/stop/jump/land at varied frame intervals.
- Coordinated weight transfer, pelvis/torso counter-rotation, articulated legs,
  takeoff/landing transitions and bounded secondary motion should replace rigid
  pose changes. Foot contact and weapon grips must remain consistent.
- Source movement already implements coyote time, jump buffering, variable jump,
  slide/hop and air control. Do not duplicate these or quietly change physics.
  Audit native access to these mechanics and snapshot-driven visual responsiveness.
- FPS kick presentation is currently a rigid procedural shin/boot assembly.
  Replace it with detailed articulated geometry and timed chamber, extension,
  contact and recovery. Chain separately accepted kick presses while preserving
  authoritative cooldown, hit-once and replay dedup semantics. Visual sequence
  variation must not be represented as unimplemented bonus damage.
- The Flash report's Fighting landing-latch/proposed-combo status may be historical;
  do not reopen it as a current defect without checking current code and the
  previously recorded 54-case native combo evidence.
