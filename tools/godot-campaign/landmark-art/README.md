# Rootfall relay wreck and Crown receiver

`build.py` generates two original editable Blender sources in `blend/` and three-material, joined game GLBs in `godot/campaign/art/landmarks/`. Run Blender 4.5.14 with one thread:

```sh
LP_NUM_THREADS=1 /home/mojo/.tmp-on-disk/cocs-blender-toolchain/blender-4.5.14-linux-x64/blender -b -t 1 --python tools/godot-campaign/landmark-art/build.py
```

The mesh coordinates use the existing recipe transforms. Rootfall's 26 × 4 × 4 prop becomes a roughly 20 m tapering **triangular** lattice wreck (open side faces, three longitudinal chords, staggered shear bracing, bulky riveted junctions, torn end plates, bent aerial and ceramic feed). Its left end bends south away from the encounter-1 flank path and is placed at the real terrain height instead of the old 3 m hover; the central saddle meets the already-blocked relay cabin roof; the right end rests against the rising slope. The occupied scenery remains several metres from the nearest flank route. Its visual meshes add no collision.

Crown's 30 m primary dish is an **open** parabolic rib-and-reflector assembly. Separated outer reflector petals retain readable receiver machinery without the opaque underside that appeared as a floating rock. A bounded open service sector avoids intersections with the three tall existing Crown fins through the southern face. The two bearing shoes and splayed compression trusses touch the tops of the *existing* `court-buttress-4-±1` blocked columns; the focal feed is held by three stays. Lower support is above any routed player's head. All four smaller `dish` props elsewhere retain their original representation.

`terrain.gd` selects these two authored scenes by original prop kind/scale. The generated recipe, coordinates, routes, terrain triangles, objective anchors and authoritative block collisions are unchanged. Three merged material surfaces per model avoid component-level draw calls. The blend files preserve separately named editable parts; GLB source regeneration is deterministic. Imported final meshes are 1,568 triangles (wreck) and 1,888 triangles (receiver), each three surfaces; redundant automatic imported mesh LOD and shadow mesh generation is disabled.
